# Support more than one language, through a single language table

The app launched Telugu-only, with "no multi-language code paths" as a locked rule. In October 2026 we lifted that rule and added Hindi as a pilot. The reason was a Telugu heritage-language app that had already won the same Congressional App Challenge district in 2024: a Telugu-only entry would look derivative, and the problem it solves (understanding a family language but being unable to speak it) is shared by every immigrant family, not just Telugu ones. To keep this from spreading conditionals through the codebase, everything that differs per language lives in one table, `packages/core/src/languages.ts`: the persona's name, the register, the scripted lines and the voice. Topics carry their own language code, and nothing else branches on language. Adding a third language means adding a profile, its topic files and a voice.
