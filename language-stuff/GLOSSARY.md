# Heritage-language speaking practice

Short voice conversations that help people who understand their family's language start speaking it, with an AI grandmother who never corrects them.

## People

**Receptive bilingual**:
Someone who understands a language when it is spoken to them but cannot speak it back. The only user this app is for.
_Avoid_: learner, student, beginner

**Grandmother persona**:
The AI character the user talks to: a maternal grandmother who speaks the topic's language. She has a different name in each language (Ammamma in Telugu, Nani in Hindi).
_Avoid_: tutor, teacher, bot, assistant

**Persona name**:
The word for maternal grandmother in a given language. It is part of the language, not the brand, so it appears in that language's dialogue as-is; English copy writes it as `{persona}`.

**Native reviewer**:
A native speaker who has read a topic end to end and approved it. Only their approval makes a topic verified.

## Content

**Language**:
One of the languages the app can practise (Telugu, Hindi). Each has a launch status: a full corpus or a pilot.
_Avoid_: locale

**Pilot language**:
A language with a handful of topics, checked for structure but not held to the full corpus size or emotional balance.

**Topic**:
One conversation scenario of 5–8 turns, in one language, anchored to a relationship.
_Avoid_: lesson, exercise, unit

**Turn**:
One exchange in a topic: her line, and the answer the user is working towards.

**Model answer**:
The answer a turn is built around, shown in native script, romanization and English. Shown in full on Easy and with gaps on Medium.
_Avoid_: correct answer, right answer

**Key vocab**:
The words a turn is teaching, written as `native (romanization) — meaning`.

**Emotional anchor**:
The relationship a topic is about: grandparent, parent, cousin, or the wider culture.

**Starter topic**:
The one topic per language that a new user drops into straight after onboarding.

**Verified**:
A topic a native reviewer has approved. Only verified topics are ever seen by users.

## Practice

**Session**:
One sitting with one topic, capped at five minutes, ending with her closing line.
_Avoid_: lesson, attempt

**Difficulty**:
How much of the model answer is shown: Easy (all of it), Medium (with gaps), Hard (none).
_Avoid_: level

**Understood**:
Her judgement that the user's reply plausibly answered her. Deliberately generous; never shown to the user as a score.
_Avoid_: correct, passed

**Re-ask**:
Asking the same question again in simpler words when a reply was not understood, as if she had not heard. At most once per turn.

**Spoken word**:
A key-vocab word that was actually in the transcript of the user's speech. Collected in "Words you've said".
_Avoid_: learned word, mastered word, score

**Voice note**:
A line the user records at the end of a session to send to their real grandmother through the phone's share sheet. It never leaves the phone unless they send it.
